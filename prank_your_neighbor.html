Prank Your Neighbor: Rules
All players get one card. Everyone can either choose to keep the card they have, or trade with the player to their left.
You can only see your own card, so you don't know what you will get for trading.
After all players have decided to "stay" or "trade", everyone will reveal their card. The player with the lowest card loses a life.
Card Ranking: King > Queen > Jack > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 > Ace
