HOW TO PLAY
Goal: Be the last player standing!
Setup: Each player gets 1 hidden card. You start with 3 lives.
Trading: Choose to TRADE your card with the player on your left, or STAY with your card.
King Block: If your neighbor has a King, the trade is blocked!
Reveal: After all trades, cards are revealed.
Losing: The player with the lowest card loses 1 life.
Card Ranking: King > Queen > Jack > 10-2 > Ace
